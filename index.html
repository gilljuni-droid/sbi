Pillars of Formation

Intellectual Formation

Saint Benedict valued study as a form of service to God. Intellectual formation develops critical thinking, lifelong learning, and professional competence so that knowledge can be used responsibly for the good of others.

Human Formation

Saint Benedict teaches discipline, humility, obedience, hospitality, and balance in daily life. Human formation helps students cultivate good character, emotional maturity, integrity, and healthy relationships through faithful work and community living.

Spiritual Formation

The "Ora" (Pray) dimension reminds students that prayer is the foundation of life. Benedictine spirituality encourages regular prayer, participation in the sacraments, reflection, and seeking God's presence in both worship and everyday activities.

Pastoral Formation

The "Labora" (Work) dimension emphasizes that work is an expression of love and service. Pastoral formation encourages students to become servant leaders who actively participate in evangelization, community outreach, social responsibility, and the mission of the Church.

Outreach Program

Student Catechists

Since its establishment, Saint Benedict Institute (SBI), up to the last academic year, continuously extended its mission of evangelization through community service by sending its students to Cabittaogan Elementary School and Cabittaogan National High School. Under the pastoral care of the Parish of Saint Catherine (Sta. Catalina), SBI students served as volunteer catechists, assisting in the teaching of Religious Education and Catechism classes.

At present, the institution has redirected this catechetical outreach to Pangada-Cabaroan Integrated School, while remaining under the pastoral jurisdiction of the Parish of Saint Catherine (Sta. Catalina). Pangada-Cabaroan Integrated School This continuing partnership enables SBI students to carry out the Church's mission of evangelization by providing catechetical instruction and values formation to elementary and secondary learners.

Through this ministry, SBI students help deepen the learners' understanding of the Catholic faith, Christian values, prayer, the Sacraments, and the teachings of the Church. At the same time, the outreach serves as a practical training ground where future educators and catechists apply the knowledge and skills they have acquired in the classroom. It strengthens their competencies in teaching, leadership, communication, and pastoral service while fostering a spirit of compassion, responsibility, and missionary discipleship.

This catechetical program reflects SBI's unwavering commitment to forming servant leaders and faith-filled educators who actively participate in the evangelizing mission of the Church by bringing Christ's message to communities within the Parish of Saint Catherine (Sta. Catalina). Through this sustained partnership with local schools, the institution continues to embody its vision of integrating academic excellence with Christian service, responding to the spiritual and moral needs of young people in the community.
